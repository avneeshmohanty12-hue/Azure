A little about us

Fresh bakes, with a thoughtful point of view.

Azure Bakery is a home bakery founded with a simple idea: bakery treats can be delicious, welcoming and made with more thoughtful ingredient choices. We bring together the joy of fresh baking and alternatives that feature oats, millets, ragi and whole grains.

We believe in baking in small batches, paying attention to freshness and being clear about the ingredients used. Selected recipes use jaggery or very little added sugar; ask us about the sweetener used in each product. Every order receives personal attention, because the little details make a difference.

Rooted in good choices

Celebrating Odisha’s ragi revival.

Odisha’s Shree Anna Abhiyan, formerly known as the Odisha Millets Mission, works to revive millet cultivation and consumption, with ragi as an important focus. Inspired by this effort and Odisha’s food traditions, Azure Bakery brings ragi into familiar bakery recipes in a fresh, enjoyable way. Azure Bakery is an independent home bakery and is not affiliated with the government programme.
